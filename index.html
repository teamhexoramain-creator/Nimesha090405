FAQ

Godak aya ahana prashna

Project ekak iwara karanna kochchara kal yanawada?

Logo ekakata dawas 3k witara, reel ekakata dawas 1 – 2k. Podi app ekakata sathi 4 – 6 k, loku systems walata masa 2 – 4 k yanna puluwan. Project form eke oyage project ekata galapena time eka pennanawa.

Patan ganna mata mokada ona?

Oyage idea eka witharai. Mokada ona, kawda use karanne kiyala kiyanna. Oyata kamathi apps, logos, videos thiyenawa nam ewath ewanna. Design saha technical wada okkoma api karanawa.

Play Store / App Store ekata danna puluwanda?

Ow. Listing eka, screenshots, builds okkoma hadala oyage developer account eken publish karanawa. Ethakota app eka oyage namin thiyenne. Google USD 25 ekaparai, Apple USD 99 awuruddata.

Source code eka mata labenawada?

Ow. Final payment eka kalama full source code eka saha project eke logins okkoma oyata denawa.

Launch karata passe mokada wenne?

1 month ekak bug fixes free. Ita passe updates, aluth features, server checks walata monthly maintenance plan ekak ganna puluwan.

Logo ekakata revisions kiyak denawada?

Concepts 3k pennanawa. Oyata kamathi eka revisions 3k wenakan free. Final files PNG, JPG, SVG, PDF widiyata denawa, print walatai online walatai danna puluwan.

Video editing walata footage ewanne kohomada?

Google Drive link ekak hari WhatsApp eken ewanna puluwan. Reel ekak usually dawas 1 – 2kin, YouTube video ekak dawas 2 – 4kin deliver karanawa.

Payment karanne kohomada?

Wada patan ganna 50% advance, ithuru tika deliver karaddi, bank transfer eken. Loku projects milestones walata kadala gewanna puluwan.

Project ekak thiyenawada?

App ekak, logo ekak, video ekak, mokak unath prashna tikakata uththara dila minute 2kin estimate price ekak ganna. Salli ona na, commitment ekak na.
